# Pegasus Surveillance of Journalists and Human Rights Defenders in Jordan

**TLP:CLEAR** — Bilingual CTI report (EN/AR) analyzing the Pegasus spyware campaign against journalists, lawyers, activists, and human rights defenders in Jordan, using the Diamond Model of Intrusion Analysis and the MITRE ATT&CK framework.

## About this report

Between 2019 and 2021, at least four Jordanian journalists, lawyers, and human rights defenders had their devices compromised with Pegasus spyware, operated by two locally-tracked actors, **MANSAF** and **BLACKIRIS**. A wider 2024 investigation later confirmed the real scale of the campaign was closer to 35 targeted individuals.

This report maps the full campaign — adversary, infrastructure, capability, and victims — against the Diamond Model, cross-references the observed techniques with MITRE ATT&CK, and provides a structured intelligence assessment with confidence levels and documented gaps.

## Methodology

- **Diamond Model of Intrusion Analysis** — adversary / infrastructure / capability / victim
- **MITRE ATT&CK** — tactic and technique mapping with per-technique confidence assessment
- **Structured analytic technique** — words of estimative probability (High / Medium / Low confidence) applied throughout, following intelligence community tradecraft standards
